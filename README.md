# AiSCheck for Android

> Uses the excellently curated AiSList to quickly determine if a YouTube channel is AI nonsense or not

## What is AiSCheck

This is an incredibly straightforward and small Android app that keeps a locally cached version of the wonderful [AiSList](https://github.com/Override92/AiSList) and lets you quickly see if a YouTube channel is on either the Block List or the Warn List by pasting in the URL or just the channel name. I understand you can just keep a local clone of that repo on your phone and simply search it in a text editor of your choice so I also added the ability to use the YouTube apps Share feature to share channels straight with AiSCheck.

 - 🔴 If a channel is on the Block List, you see a red alert
 - 🟠 If a channel is on the Warn List, you see an orange warning
 - 🟢 If a channel is on neither list, you see a green message

Simple as that. There are no ads or tracking or anything inside this app, it's just a simple lookup that will keep those lists up-to-date.
